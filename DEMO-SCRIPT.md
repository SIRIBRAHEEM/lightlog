# LightLog demo video script (about 2 minutes)

Record the screen at desktop size, then one short mobile clip at the
end if time allows. Speak slowly and clearly. Total target: 2 minutes.

## 0:00 - 0:15 Intro (welcome screen)

Show the welcome screen.

Say: "Hello, I am Ibrahim Yahaya from Bida, Niger State, Nigeria, a
student at the Federal University of Technology Minna. This is
LightLog. If you are a student where I live, your day depends on when
NEPA brings light. LightLog stops the guessing. It tracks your
electricity and tells you when to charge."

Click: "Try it with a sample week".

## 0:15 - 0:45 Today screen

Show the status card, the three stat tiles, and the timeline.

Say: "This is the Today screen. It shows whether light is on right
now and for how long, how much light I have had today, and every
change I logged. When the light changes, I tap one button."

Click: the big toggle button once, so the state flips and the timeline
gains a new entry. Point at the new timeline row.

Say: "If I noticed a change late, I can also add it with the real
time it happened." (Scroll briefly to the "Missed a change?" card,
do not fill it in.)

## 0:45 - 1:15 Patterns screen

Click the "Patterns" tab.

Say: "After a few days of logging, patterns appear. Each bar is one
hour of the day. Taller means light is more likely at that hour. In
this sample week, evenings are reliable, around 9pm is 97 percent.
Below, I can see how many hours of light I got each day this week,
about 6 hours a day on average."

Hover or point at one tall bar and at the "most reliable hours" chips.

## 1:15 - 1:45 Charge planner

Click the "Charge planner" tab.

Say: "The charge planner is my favourite part. I pick my device, set
my battery level, and it tells me how long a full charge needs and
the best time to start, based on my own light pattern."

Click: Laptop, drag the battery slider to about 20 percent.

Say: "My laptop at 20 percent needs about two hours. LightLog says
the best window starts at 9pm, when light usually holds. If light is
already on, it just tells me to plug in now."

## 1:45 - 2:00 Closing

Go back to the Today screen.

Say: "Everything is saved on the device, no account needed. That is
LightLog. Thank you for watching."

End on the Today screen with the logo visible.
